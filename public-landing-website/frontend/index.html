Frequently asked questions

Who can join?

Students from Class 5 onwards, college learners, and working professionals. Each program lists its audience.

Are classes online or offline?

Both. Each course card shows whether it runs online, offline or hybrid.

What are the fees and batch timings?

Fees and timings differ by program. Send an enquiry and a counsellor will share the current details.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Completed programs come with a recognized certificate that employers can verify.

Do you help with internships and jobs?

Yes. See Internships and Industry Projects and Career and Placement Support above.

Our Mission & Vision

OD AI HUB is an AI talent-to-innovation platform empowering students, professionals, institutions and businesses to learn, build, innovate and scale. We believe that talent in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities in Odisha holds tremendous potential that, with the right industry alignment and mentorship, can compete globally.
